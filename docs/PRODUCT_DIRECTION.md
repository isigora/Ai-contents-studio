# Product direction — user clarification, 2026-10-05

This addendum preserves Master_Development_Specification.md unchanged. It records the
user's clarified final product, not an assertion that these capabilities are shipped.

## Customer value

Each customer supplies short keywords or updates permitted business materials. Their AI
organises the facts, requests essential missing details and creates text, image campaigns,
video and subtitles. Connected channels receive approved or explicitly delegated posts.
Customers and suppliers each have an agent that detects needs and available supply in
authorised data, exchanges structured proposals and reports opportunities to its owner.

The owner's interaction should be brief; a long manual knowledge form is not the final
experience. The current four-field quick entry is a factual, low-cost bridge, not an AI
document interpreter. Unknown prices, quantities, claims and delivery terms stay unknown.

## Phases and evidence

- P0: login and factual knowledge/content workflow. User reported preview login success
  on 2026-10-05. Workspace, editing and mobile browser gates remain to be demonstrated.
- P1: reusable image/video/subtitle production, approval, languages and channel templates.
  X is the user's first selected channel. X text templates do not connect an account.
- P2: official X account connection, approved publishing, scheduling and outcome tracking.
  Channel selection is resolved. Account authorisation, API budget and operating callback
  domain are not resolved. No paid calls or real posts are authorised by channel selection.
- P3: consented need detection → matching → agent condition exchange → owner notification
  → approved/delegated transaction. No matching or trading implementation exists yet.

## Proposed agent protocol requirements (not yet implemented)

1. Agents identify the owner/workspace, purpose and permission scope for each exchange.
2. Shared need/offer records carry item/service, specification, quantity, price/currency,
   delivery window, region, evidence, expiry and unknown fields. No inferred fact is marked
   confirmed until the owner or a trusted source confirms it.
3. Exchanges have version, conversation/request IDs, consent scope and an audit trail.
   Keep private documents, credentials and nonconsented customer data out of messages.
4. Agents can ask questions and compare conditions within the owner's delegation.
   Reports explain candidate, fit, proposed conditions, uncertainty and next action.
5. Automatic ordering/payment/contract acceptance requires separately configured authority.
   The initial capability recommends and notifies; human approval is the default.
6. Detection freshness depends on connected sources and refresh schedules; do not claim
   continuous real-time sensing without a running source integration and measured latency.

## Immediate sequence

Complete the factual preview workflow, add a verified AI intake/generation provider under
a chosen budget, then implement the first X publishing flow. Expand to media posting and
scheduling before building the consented agent exchange. Keep PARTIAL phase labels until
each browser and release gate has evidence.
